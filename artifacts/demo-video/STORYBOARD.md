# OrgMemory client demo

This video is built from the running local OrgMemory product. The captures show
the real landing page, connector gateway, HCAG retrieval story, a live cited
query, source expansion, the ArcadeDB evidence graph, change intelligence,
Memory Work, approvals, and the audit log.

## Narrative

1. The problem: company knowledge is fragmented.
2. The wedge: connect once and serve governed context to every agent.
3. The engine: specialist retrieval, current-truth reconciliation, and cited
   context envelopes.
4. The proof: run a real query and inspect its exact GitHub sources.
5. The moat: a longitudinal evidence graph that remembers why facts are true.
6. The control plane: changes refresh context, and consequential writes require
   approval and produce audit records.
7. The positioning: OrgMemory complements Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT, and Codex.

The final MP4 uses subtle motion, crossfades, branded callouts, an original
ambient bed, and locally generated narration. `captions.srt` is included as a
sidecar for accessible playback and future edits.
